FAQ - Hood Maxxers Frequently Asked Questions
Help Support
1. What is Hood Maxxers? ▼
Hood Maxxers is a web3 trading ecosystem and 10,000 piece generative NFT collection featuring the MAXX League: an elite trading competition where NFT holders lock their characters, activate with $MAXXER, and compete with MAXX CASH for huge reward pools!
2. What is the $MAXXER token? ▼
$MAXXER is the core utility token powering the entire ecosystem. Its primary function: $MAXXER activates MAXXERS. Every activation requires 10,000 $MAXXER (50% funds the competition prize pool, and 50% is permanently burned). NO $MAXXER, NO MAXX IN.
3. What is "MAXX IN"? ▼
MAXX IN is the locking mechanism where you lock your Hood Maxxer NFT and pay the 10,000 $MAXXER activation fee to receive your starting $10,000 MAXX CASH trading allocation. Your NFT remains locked safely in the vault for the duration of the competition.
4. What is MAXX CASH? ▼
MAXX CASH is the competitive in-game trading balance assigned to activated MAXXERS. It is used on the Dashboard to trade simulated stock tokens like $AAPL, $TSLA, $NVDA, $GOOG, $AMZN, and $MSFT to climb the leaderboard!
5. How does the MAXX League trading competition work? ▼
Traders execute buy and sell orders on stock token pairs. At the end of Season 1, the top 300 traders on the leaderboard win shares of the MAXX POOL prize fund and unlock exclusive rewards via MAXX OUT.
6. How do I get on the Whitelist? ▼
Open the Whitelist Application from your desktop or taskbar, complete the 5 community tasks (Follow on X, Like/Repost, Tag 3 Maxxers, Join Discord, and submit your wallet address), and generate your verifiable holographic pass!
7. When does the official launch take place? ▼
Our official first post and announcements go live this Monday! Keep an eye on our official X (@HoodMaxxers) and Discord channels for mint dates and competition kickoff times.
